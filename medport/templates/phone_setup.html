MedPort on your phone

Three steps, once. Keep this phone on the same Wi-Fi as your computer.

  1. Let this phone trust MedPort on your Wi-Fi

    Phones only run offline, encrypted apps over a secure connection. MedPort made its own certificate for your home network. It only works for home-network addresses (like 192.168.x.x), so it can't be used to pretend to be any real website.

    {{ icon("download") }}Download the certificate
    1. Open Settings and search for CA certificate. On most phones it's under Security & privacy → More security settings → Encryption & credentials → Install a certificate.
    2. Choose CA certificate, tap Install anyway, and pick medport-home-wifi.crt from Downloads.

    To remove it later: Settings → Encryption & credentials → User credentials (or Trusted credentials → User) → MedPort → Remove.

    1. Open this page in Safari, tap the download button, then Allow.
    2. Open Settings → Profile Downloaded → Install.
    3. Then Settings → General → About → Certificate Trust Settings, and switch on MedPort home Wi-Fi.

    To remove it later: Settings → General → VPN & Device Management → MedPort → Remove Profile.

    Check it's yours: the certificate's SHA-256 fingerprint starts with {{ fingerprint }}.

  2. Open the app and add it to your home screen

    {{ icon("logo") }}Open MedPort app

    Android (Chrome): menu ⋮ → Install app or Add to Home screen.
    iPhone (Safari): Share → Add to Home Screen. Open it from the home screen from now on.

    If the app says the connection isn't private, step 1 isn't finished yet.

  3. Pair it

    On your computer, open Share & backup → Phone app → Pair a phone. Scan that QR code, or type its code into the app, then choose a PIN.
